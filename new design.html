Bringing together believers, leaders, and a new generation of Kingdom ambassadors to be equipped, empowered, and commissioned to take the world for Christ.
The Mantle is a special four-day experience within the Holy Ghost Convention, established through God's mandate to Bishop Francis Wale Oke to raise and ignite a new generation for His Kingdom.

Open to everyone with a heart to serve God's purpose, The Mantle equips, refreshes, empowers and commissions believers for greater impact in ministry and everyday life.
